# Review Architecture Notes

Review architecture notes preserve review-scope and evidence decisions that Test and Ship must understand.

Required fields:

- role: `Staff Reviewer`
- decisions: review target, scope boundaries, and evidence trust decisions
- constraints: diff shape, missing evidence, generated output, source, release, or artifact-state constraints
- tradeoffs: pass, pass-with-risk, or hold tradeoffs
- assumptions: assumptions Test or Ship must verify
- downstream_impact: required fixes, verification focus, release gates, or follow-up work
