# Reflect Architecture Notes

Reflect architecture notes preserve durable process and follow-up decisions.

Required fields:

- role: `Project Manager`
- decisions: what to learn, defer, follow up, or leave unchanged
- constraints: missing evidence, waivers, release state, source handoff, review gaps, or verification gaps
- tradeoffs: learning candidate, follow-up, or curation tradeoffs
- assumptions: assumptions future lifecycle runs should verify
- downstream_impact: future skills, templates, config, validators, docs, or follow-up artifacts
