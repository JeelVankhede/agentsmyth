# Ship Architecture Notes

Ship architecture notes preserve release and handoff decisions that Reflect must understand.

Required fields:

- role: `Senior DevOps`
- decisions: ship, hold, hold-with-waiver, release, rollback, and handoff decisions
- constraints: release config, verification evidence, PR/CI state, source handling, access, or required gate constraints
- tradeoffs: accepted, deferred, blocked, or waived risk tradeoffs
- assumptions: assumptions Reflect must preserve or call out
- downstream_impact: release, rollback, source handoff, blocked handoff, or follow-up impact
