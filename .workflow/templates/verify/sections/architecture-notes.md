# Verify Architecture Notes

Verify architecture notes preserve evidence decisions that Ship must understand.

Required fields:

- role: `Senior QA`
- decisions: evidence type, coverage mapping, and rerun or waiver decisions
- constraints: verification config, environment, tooling, generated output, source, or release constraints
- tradeoffs: command, manual, generated-output, skipped-check, or waiver tradeoffs
- assumptions: assumptions Ship must preserve, challenge, or waive
- downstream_impact: ship gate, rollback, source handoff, or follow-up impact
